Freshman Guide

How to survive your first exam season calmly.

This page is for anyone who just started university and doesn't know where to begin. Only simple tips that you can really follow.

See the study plan

6 tips for the exam season

Start early

Begin preparing in advance, not the night before the exam.

One folder

Put all lecture notes in one folder so you don't have to search later.

One subject a day

Studying one subject per day causes less confusion.

Sleep well

Sleep at least 7 hours, or you will forget what you learned.

Take breaks

Rest for 10 minutes after every hour of studying.

Ask for help

If something is unclear, ask your classmates or the teacher.

2-week study plan

14–10 days before
Collect notes and make a list of exam questions.
9–5 days before
Read and learn the topics from the list.
4–2 days before
Review hard topics and solve practice tasks.
1 day before
Quickly look through everything and go to bed early.

Hint: print this plan and put it above your desk.
